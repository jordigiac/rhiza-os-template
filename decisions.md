# Decisions — what was decided, and why

Newest first. When the owner decides something in a session, /handoff records
it here the same day. The "why" matters more than the "what" — future-you
will want the reasoning, not just the outcome.

**Format:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD — Short title

**Decision:** what was decided.

**Why:** the reasoning, in a sentence or two.
```
