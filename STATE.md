### How this board stays honest

- **Nothing is ever deleted.** Finished and dropped work gets marked and
  dated, and leaves the board only when the owner confirms.
- **Nothing is invented.** Rows come from what the owner actually said.
- **One line per thread.** The next-step cell answers what happens next and
  what's holding it, in fifty words or fewer. History belongs in decisions.md
  and journal.md. A board that grows into a diary is a board nobody reads.
- **Every state is a proposal**, corrected by the owner saying so.
