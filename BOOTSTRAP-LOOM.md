# The bootstrap Loom — shot list and script

*(Production material for Jordi, not client-facing. One recording, reused
for every client. It covers everything before the system takes over:
claim, Claude, the magic line. Target: under five minutes. Record against
a clean browser profile with no personal tabs or account names visible.)*

## Before recording

- A test GitHub account signed OUT (you sign in on camera as "the client")
- A test repo transfer already initiated, so the invitation email is
  sitting in the test inbox
- Claude Desktop uninstalled, so the download is real
- Screen clean: one browser window, no extra tabs

## The shots

**Shot 1 — the welcome (20 seconds).**
Screen: the welcome email a client receives.
Say: "When your payment lands, you get this email. It has your setup
sheet, this video, and one question: your GitHub username. Here's the
whole thing, start to finish, about ten minutes, and I'll do it exactly
as you will."

**Shot 2 — the GitHub account (60 seconds).**
Screen: github.com → Sign up → free plan → done.
Say: "GitHub is where your system lives. Think of it as the deed to the
house: the account is yours, so the system is yours. Free plan, two
minutes, all in the browser. Reply to the welcome email with this
username and I'll send your system over."

**Shot 3 — accepting the transfer (30 seconds).**
Screen: the invitation email → accept → the repo page with THEIR name at
the top.
Say: "GitHub emails you an invitation. One click. See the name at the
top? That's your account. From this second the system belongs to you,
not me."

**Shot 4 — the Claude account and app (90 seconds).**
Screen: claude.ai → sign up → pick Pro → claude.ai/download → install →
sign in.
Say: "Claude is the engine. The Pro plan is twenty a month, between you
and Claude directly, cancel anytime. Then the desktop app: download,
install, sign in. If anything snags here, that's a text-me moment, not a
figure-it-out moment."

**Shot 5 — the magic line (60 seconds).**
Screen: Claude open → paste the setup line from the welcome email → Claude
working → the system's first reply.
Say: "Now the good part. Your welcome email has one line in it. Paste it
into Claude, press enter, and watch: Claude pulls your system onto this
computer and hands you over to it. From here you're not following steps
anymore, you're just talking. About twenty minutes from now it knows
your business. I'll see you at the workshop."

- The video never names another client and never shows your own accounts;
  if either happens, cut or re-record the shot.
