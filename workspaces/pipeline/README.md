# pipeline/ — everyone who might become a client

This folder holds your leads when they don't live in a tool you already
use. It's one plain table, `leads.md`, made the first time you mention
someone who might buy. Each person has where they came from, where things
stand, and one next step with a date, so nobody falls through the cracks.

**If you already keep leads somewhere** (ClickUp, a CRM, Notion, a
spreadsheet), that's the better home, because it's where you already look.
Say "help me connect ClickUp" (or whichever tool) and the system walks you
through it. Once it's connected, your leads go there instead, and this
folder stays empty. Your leads only ever live in one place.

Follow-up drafts for leads also land here, as `follow-up.md`. Nothing is
ever sent for you. You send it.
