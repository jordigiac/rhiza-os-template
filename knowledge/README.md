# knowledge/ — what this OS knows about the business

The always-true facts: what the business is, who runs it, how it sounds, and
what matters this quarter. Filled during onboarding, in the owner's own words.

Starts as a handful of flat files. When one gets crowded (a growing team, more
than one department), it gets split into a folder with a small index during a
monthly maintenance pass — the structure grows with the business.

One home per fact: if something is written here, other files point to it
instead of repeating it.

**Every fact file opens with a "Last confirmed:" line** — the date a human
last said its contents are still true. Live capture and the handoff sweep
refresh it when facts change; the checkup refreshes the oldest; the audit
counts the ages. No date means never confirmed — first in line at the next
checkup.
