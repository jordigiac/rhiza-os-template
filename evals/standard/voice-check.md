# Voice check — does it sound like you?

Runs on: any draft meant for a person to read (emails, posts, messages,
documents). When: every time, before the owner sees it.

Pass conditions — all must pass:

- Passed through the humanizer skill; no AI tells survive.
- Reads like `knowledge/core/voice.md` — the register of the owner's own pasted
  examples, not a press release.
- Nothing was sent, posted, or published. Drafts stay drafts until the
  owner's explicit yes.

Verdict: PASS or FAIL, plus a one-line critique (what's off, even on a pass).
