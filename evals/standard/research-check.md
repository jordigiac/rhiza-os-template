# Research check — is it true, or is it plausible?

Runs on: any "go find out" task about the outside world — a competitor, a
tool, a market, a person, a partner, a price. When: every time.

Pass conditions — all must pass:

- **Every claim traces to a source that was actually opened this run.** A
  search result summary is not a source. Open the page.
- **Fact, the source's opinion, and this system's inference stay visibly
  separate.** Label them. A vendor's marketing claim is not a fact about
  their product.
- **Coverage honesty.** Say what wasn't searched and what couldn't be
  reached, out loud, in the result.
- **It ends with "what would change this conclusion."**
- **The output fits the ask.** A quick question gets a short answer with its
  source. A real investigation gets the findings, the gaps, and the sources.

Verdict: PASS or FAIL, plus a one-line critique.

*Why this exists: confidently inventing a source is the failure this system
is most likely to have, and it is the one the owner is least able to catch.*
