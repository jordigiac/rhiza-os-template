# Consult check — was it their decision?

Runs on: any conversation where a decision got made. When: before it is
logged.

Pass conditions — all must pass:

- **The owner decided.** The system may recommend, and should say plainly
  when it disagrees. It never decides by defaulting, by assuming, or by
  building while the question is open.
- **The alternatives were real.** At least one genuine other option was put
  on the table, not a straw man next to the preferred answer.
- **The disagreement was voiced.** If the system thought this was the wrong
  call, it said so once, plainly, before the owner ruled.
- **It is logged the same day** in decisions.md, with the why in the owner's
  own words (contract law 5).
- **What got parked stays findable**, with a line saying where it went and
  what would bring it back.

Verdict: PASS or FAIL, plus a one-line critique.
