# Your OS setup call — what we'll do together

**Time:** about 90 minutes, one sitting. You talk; Jordi drives the keyboard.

## What we'll cover

1. **Your business, in your words** — what you do, who you serve, who does
   what. No prep needed; you already know all the answers.
2. **How you sound** — you'll share a couple of real things you've written,
   so your system writes drafts that sound like you, not like AI.
3. **What matters right now** — your top priorities for the next 90 days.
4. **Your tools** — where your email, calendar, customers, tasks, and money
   live. Just names; connecting them is Jordi's job, after the call.
5. **The painful stuff** — the 2–3 things you do over and over that eat your
   time. This is the valuable part: we'll walk through each one and score
   which are worth automating first.

## What you leave with

- A working operating system that knows your business, your voice, and your
  priorities — you can talk to it that same day.
- **1–3 automations picked, with the first build scheduled** — you'll see it
  in your system's "Up next" list, with a date.

## How to prepare

Nothing formal. Have handy: a couple of recent things you wrote (emails are
perfect), and honest examples of work that keeps eating your week. Concrete
beats polished — the messier the example, the better the automation.
