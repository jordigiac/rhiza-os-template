# Your monthly tune-up — what it covers

Once a month, Jordi services your operating system the way a mechanic
services a car — a few hours of maintenance, and you get the report.

## What gets checked

- **Everything still true?** The system's map is checked against reality —
  stale files, contradictions, and clutter get flagged and fixed.
- **Automations ran clean?** Every automation's logs get reviewed; anything
  on the trust ladder that earned a promotion gets promoted.
- **Skills still working?** Anything unused or broken gets fixed or retired.
- **System still fits the business?** As you grow, your knowledge files get
  reorganized so the system stays fast and cheap to run.

## What you get

- **The tune-up report** — plain English: what was checked, what was fixed,
  what changed, how your system scored.
- **A refreshed "Up next" list** — what's being built for you and when to
  expect it.

## What it costs you

Nothing extra — the tune-up is part of the retainer. Your time cost is zero;
questions that need your answer arrive as a short list, answerable by text.
