# Your business's operating system

This is a folder of plain text files that turns Claude into the part of your
business that remembers everything.

Not a chatbot you re-explain yourself to every morning. A system that knows
what you sell, who you serve, how you sound, what you launched last month and
what you promised a partner for next Tuesday. You talk to it, it does the
work, and everything it learns stays in files you own.

Built by [Rhiza Systems](https://rhizasystems.com). Free to use, yours to keep.

## What it does once it knows your business

- Drafts your launch emails, posts and landing copy in your voice, from what
  it already knows about the offer.
- Keeps the launch straight: what goes out when, which link goes where, what
  each partner still needs from you.
- Holds your programs, your clients and your JV partners as files it reads
  before it writes anything.
- Remembers every decision you make and why, so next quarter's you can ask.
- Saves itself to your private GitHub every hour, so nothing is ever only in
  a chat window.

## What you need

- **Claude**, on an account that can run either Claude Code or the Claude

- **A GitHub account** (free). This is where your copy lives and backs itself up.

No coding. If you can rename a folder, you can run this.

## Start here

1. **Make your own private copy.** Click **Use this template** at the top of
   this page, choose **Private**, and name it after your business. Do not fork
   or clone this repo directly, because you will not be able to save your work
   back to it.
2. **Get it on your computer and open it.** The full walkthrough, with the
   downloads, lives in [`setup/START-HERE.md`](setup/START-HERE.md).
3. **Say hey.** Open the folder in Claude and type "hey". It introduces itself
   and starts the setup conversation. That takes about forty minutes, and you
   can stop and come back whenever you want.

When setup finishes, the `setup/` folder deletes itself and you are running.

## What is in here

| Folder | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `knowledge/core/` | The facts every draft is built from: what you sell, who you serve, how you sound. Changes only when you say so. |
| `knowledge/` | Everything else it learns as you talk: your days, your priorities, your clients, your partners, your programs. |
| `workspaces/` | One folder per piece of real work. A launch, a partner campaign, a course build. |
| `decisions.md` | Every decision and the why, dated. |
| `STATE.md` | What is alive right now. |
| `connections/` | The tools it can reach, one file each. |
| `automations/` | What runs on its own. |
| `evals/` | The quality bar it holds itself to before anything reaches you. |
| `archive/` | Nothing is ever deleted. It gets boxed here. |

## The skills it comes with

Say what you want in plain words. It picks the right one, and you never have
to learn names.

- **onboard** teaches it your business. Runs once, at the start.
- **pickup** opens a session: where we were, what is next. Changes nothing.
- **handoff** closes one: files what you said, updates the board, saves to GitHub.
- **write-like-me** drafts anything in your voice, from real samples of your writing.
- **humanizer** strips the AI tells out of anything written for a person.
- **checkup** keeps its facts about you true, in about five minutes.
- **audit** is the monthly health check on the system itself.

## Being honest about what it is not

- **It is text, so it travels.** The files work with any AI tool. But each
  tool needs its own short setup, so switching saves you the typing, not the
  afternoon.
- **There is no phone app.** This lives on your computer.
- **It is one person's system today.** Your team cannot work in the same copy
  yet. Sharing across a company is what we are building next.
- **It does not send anything.** Emails, posts and messages get drafted and
  handed to you. Nothing leaves without you pressing send.
