# Send types: what each launch send is for

Defaults to write from. The owner's voice and habits win. Lengths are rough.
Every send has **one** job and **one** ask.

## Contents
- Before the event: invites, confirmation, reminders
- During the event: day emails, starting soon, replay
- Cart open: open, story, case study, FAQ, objections, bonus/early-bird
- Close: 24 hours, closing today, last call
- Social posts
- Partner swipe copy
- Proof and urgency rules
- Sources

## Before the event

| Send | Job | Length | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Invite 1 | Name the problem the reader feels; the free event is the next step | short–medium | Register |
| Invite 2–3 | New angle each time (a story, what they'll leave with, who it's for); last call names the date | short | Register |
| Confirmation (automation) | You're in: date, time + zone, room link, how to add to calendar | short | Save the date |
| Reminder (day before) | What they'll get, when, where | short | Show up |
| Starting soon (15–60 min before) | We're live soon, here's the link | 2–3 lines | Click to join |

## During the event

| Send | Job | Length | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day n (challenge) | Today's task or theme + live time | short | Show up / do the task |
| Replay | Missed it? Here it is, and how long it's up | short | Watch the replay |

## Cart open

| Send | Job | Length | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cart open | The offer is here: what it is, who it's for, price, deadline(s). Announces, doesn't push | short–medium | See the details |
| Diagnose the problem | Show you understand their situation. Most launch copy should be about the reader, not the offer | medium | Read / consider |
| Story / case study | One specific before-and-after, from a real client the owner named | medium | See if it fits you |
| FAQ | Logistics, and underneath, "will this work for me?" | medium | Ask a question / join |
| What's holding you back | Name the biggest objection, sit with it, then answer it | medium | Join |
| Bonus / early bird ends | The real deadline and what goes away | short | Join before [deadline] |

## Close

| Send | Job | Length | Ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| 24 hours left | Exact close time + zone | short | Join |
| Closing today (morning) | Doors close tonight at [time zone] | short | Join |
| Last call (final hours) | Urgency, not information | very short | Join now |

## Social posts

One idea, one ask, in the owner's social register from their voice samples and voice.md. Mirrors the
big moments (invite, event day, cart open, last day). "Link in bio" when the
channel doesn't allow links.

## Partner swipe copy

Written so any partner can paste it: `[PARTNER NAME]` placeholders, the
partner's own tracking link, the event name, date, time and zone from the
facts page, and a plain disclosure line ("I'm an affiliate and earn a commission
if you join."). 2 versions per partner send: one story-led, one direct.

## Proof and urgency rules

- Testimonials and results only from real clients the owner has named or
  pasted. Otherwise a placeholder.
- Unusual results need what typical results look like, from the owner.
- "Limited spots" only with a real seat cap in the facts page. Deadlines only as
  the facts page states them. No fake timers, no "last chance" when it isn't.
