# Launch patterns: default send plans by launch type

Defaults only. The owner's own habits (knowledge/, decisions.md, CONTRACT.md, what they say)
always win. Offsets are relative to the key dates in the facts page:
**P** = promotion starts · **E** = event (E1, E2… for multi-day) · **O** = cart
opens · **EB** = early-bird deadline · **C** = cart closes. "−3d" = three days before.

## Shared rules (every type)

- **One promotional email per audience per day.** Exceptions: event days
  (reminders stack) and cart-close day (up to 3).
- **After cart opens, every sales email excludes the buyer tag.** Buyers get a
  welcome, not more pitches.
- **Registrants and non-registrants get different emails** once registration
  opens: registrants get show-up reminders, non-registrants get invites.
- Replay goes out before the first sales email that references it.
- The last email lands in the final hours before close, never after. (A
  "doors closed / waitlist" note after close is the owner's call. Offer it
  in the reply; don't put it in the plan.)
- Social posts mirror the big moments: invite, event day, cart open, last day.

## Webinar or masterclass (one live session)

| When | Email | To |
|---|---|---|
| P (or E −10d to −7d) | Invite 1 | list (minus excludes) |
| E −3d to −2d | Invite 2 | non-registrants |
| E −1d | Invite 3, last call | non-registrants |
| on signup | Confirmation (automation, not a dated send) | each registrant |
| E −1d | Reminder + what they'll get | registrants |
| E morning | Today's the day | registrants |
| E −15 to −60 min | Starting soon + room link | registrants |
| E +0 to +1d | Replay (+ cart open if O = E) | registrants |
| O | Cart open | non-registrants (registrants got it with the replay) |
| O +1d … C −2d | 1/day: story or testimonial, FAQ, objections, bonus | all non-buyers |
| EB morning + EB evening | Early-bird ends today / tonight | non-buyers |
| C −1d | 24 hours left | non-buyers |
| C morning, C afternoon, C final 1–3 hrs | Closing today ×3 | non-buyers |

Cart window: 5–10 days is typical. 5–7 sales emails is typical.

## Multi-day challenge

Invites as for a webinar. Then, for each challenge day En:

| When | Email | To |
|---|---|---|
| En morning | Day n: today's task + live time | registrants |
| En −15 to −60 min | Going live soon + room link | registrants |
| En evening (optional) | Replay + tomorrow's preview | registrants |

The pitch is usually on the last day, with cart open (O) the same day. Cart
window after a challenge: 3–5 days. Close sequence as for a webinar.

## Email-only (open/close cart, no event)

| When | Email | To |
|---|---|---|
| O −7d … O −1d | 2–3 warm-up/value emails on the offer's problem | list |
| O | Cart open | list minus excludes |
| O +1d … C −2d | 1/day: story, FAQ, objections, bonus | non-buyers |
| C −1d, C ×3 | close sequence | non-buyers |

## Paid or in-person workshop

| When | Email | To |
|---|---|---|
| P, then weekly | Ticket invites | list |
| ticket early-bird end | Early-bird ends | non-ticket-holders |
| E −7d | Logistics: venue, parking, what to bring | ticket holders |
| E −1d | See you tomorrow + address | ticket holders |
| E +1d | Thank-you + offer (if pitched at the event) | attendees |

If the offer is pitched at the event, the cart window after it follows the
webinar close sequence, sent to attendees and ticket holders.

## Short runway (less time than the pattern needs)

Keep, in this order: 1 invite (2 if there are 7+ days) → 24-hour reminder →
starting-soon reminder with the room link → replay → cart open → the last
day ×2. Drop first: extra invites, value emails, objection/FAQ emails beyond
one. Never compress by putting two promotional emails on one day, except on
close day.

## JV partner rhythm

- **Kit to partners at least 14 days before their first send** (swipe copy,
  their tracking link, dates, graphics). If that date has passed, the kit is
  due today and the owner should expect some partners to mail less.
- Partners mail inside their window, usually 2+ times. Promoting the event
  (registration page) is typical. Mailing on cart-close day is common in
  affiliate launches, but only if the facts page's window says so.
- A reminder to each partner 1–2 days before their first send date.
- A thank-you and results note after cart closes.

## Production deadlines (work backward)

| Due | Task |
|---|---|
| P −2d | Registration page live and tested (every link clicked) |
| first partner send −14d | JV kit to partners |
| each send −2d | Email loaded and scheduled in the email tool |
| E −1d | Event room tested (link, recording on, co-host) |
| O −1d | Sales page + checkout tested with a real test purchase or coupon |
| O −1d | Buyer tag automation tested (a test buyer stops getting sales emails) |
| E +0 | Replay page up before the replay email |
