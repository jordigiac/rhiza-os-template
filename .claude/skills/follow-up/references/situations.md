# Follow-up situations: what each message does

Defaults. The owner's voice and habits win. Short and personal, with at most
one ask, and only a real one. When someone fits several rows, the skill's
priority order decides which leads.

| Situation | The message's job | The ask |
|---|---|---|
| Attended, bought | Thank them; what happens next in the program; the action step from the session | Do the action step / see you at the next call |
| Attended, didn't buy | Thank them; the session's key takeaway and action step; if the owner wants, one low-key mention of the next step (a call, the offer) with facts from the facts page | Do the action step, or reply / book a call if they want more |
| No-show | No guilt; the replay if there is one (and how long it's up, from the facts page); the one thing worth watching | Watch the replay |
| Unpaid / partly paid | A kind note: what's owed (from the roster), how to pay (link from the facts page), any due date the roster has | Pay, or reply if something's wrong |
| Refund requested | Nothing salesy. Only what the owner wants to say about the refund (their decision) | None, or the owner's words |
| Lead: Contacted | Keep the promise the owner made ("here's my calendar link") | Book / reply |
| Lead: Offer made, quiet | A warm check-in, no pressure, an easy way to say no | Reply either way |
| Lead: Not now, check-back date arrived | Pick up where they left it ("you mentioned after your review…") | Reply if the timing's right |
| Won | Welcome, what happens next (from the owner or program facts) | The first step |

Rules that apply to every situation:
- A personal line only from the owner's notes; private notes shape tone, never text.
- No invented urgency, scarcity, proof or results.
- Links, prices, amounts, dates: exact, from their source.
- Warm but facts page: a real one-to-one email, not a newsletter.
