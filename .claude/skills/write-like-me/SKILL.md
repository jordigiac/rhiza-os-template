# Write like me — drafting in the owner's voice, from evidence

The voice is not a description. It is the pile of real things the owner
wrote. This skill matches the evidence, not the adjectives.

**Everything this produces is a DRAFT.** It never sends (contract law 4).
The goal is that the owner's edit takes thirty seconds instead of ten
minutes.

## Before writing a word

1. **Read `knowledge/core/voice-samples.md`.** Load the three to five
   samples closest in format to what's being written: an email for an
   email, a post for a post. Format matters more than topic.
2. **Read `knowledge/core/voice.md`** for the distilled rules. **When the
   samples and the description disagree, the samples win**, and say so out
   loud so voice.md gets fixed.
3. **Read the rest of core for the facts:** `offers.md` for the price,
   the link and the dates; `audience.md` for who's reading and the phrases
   they actually use. Never write a price or a URL from memory of a chat.
4. **If the sample bank is thin or has nothing in this format, say so
   first.** Ask for one real example before drafting: "Send me the last
   launch email you wrote and this will sound like you instead of like
   me." One real sample beats an hour of instruction.

## Writing

- **Match the patterns, not the topics.** How they open, how long their
  sentences run, whether they use contractions, how they handle a
  transition, how they close, what punctuation they lean on.
- **Steal their actual phrases.** Words the owner uses repeatedly are the
  fingerprint. So are the words they never use.
- **Anything longer than a few lines is shape, not word choice.** Where
  the point lands, how the middle moves, how it ends. Match the shape of a
  real sample of similar length.
- **Keep their register per audience.** Someone who writes warm to their
  list and blunt to partners has two voices, and `voice.md` says so.

## Before handing it over

1. **Run the humanizer.** AI tells are still AI tells in someone else's
   voice.
2. **Run the voice check** in `evals/standard/voice-check.md`.
3. **Run the Gate** in `evals/gate.md`, like anything else that reaches a
   person.
4. **Hand it over labelled DRAFT**, with one line on what it's built from
   ("matched to your last two launch emails") and one line on anything you
   had to guess.

## After they edit it

**Their edit is the most valuable thing in this system.** When the owner
rewrites a draft, ask once: "want me to add your version to your voice
samples?" On a yes, both versions go into the Rewrites section of
`knowledge/core/voice-samples.md` with one line on what changed.

That file is core, so it changes on their word (contract law 9). Asking
takes four seconds and the bank gets better every single time they write.

## Guardrails

- Never send, post, or publish. Drafts only, always.
- Never invent a price, a date, a link, or a testimonial. Facts come from
  `knowledge/core/`. If one is missing, leave a visible blank and say so.
- Never claim results the business hasn't had.
- When the owner's voice would break a promise the offer makes, say so
  before drafting, not after.
